## Synthetic service note

The fictional NX-7 bench motor is inspected every 90 days. A technician records the unit ID, isolates power, verifies zero motion, and checks the cooling fan before opening the guard. If the fan is damaged, the technician stops and asks the service lead for an approved replacement procedure. The service lead signs the inspection record after the guard is restored.
